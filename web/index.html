How it works

Three steps. Zero confusion.

No manual. No hold music. No service window. Just point and listen.

📸

Point

Aim your camera at the problem. FixGuide sees what's in front of you — the exact part, its state, and what's at risk.

🧠

Speak

Say what's going on. The AI reasons about the full picture and makes a confident call — handle it or call a pro.

🔊

Listen

Get step-by-step guidance through your earpiece. Hands free. One step at a time. Adapts if something goes wrong.

One verdict. Always clear.

No ambiguity. Ever.

Every situation gets exactly one of three answers. You always know what to do next.

✓

Handle It

You can do this. Walks you through it step by step. Adapts if something goes wrong. Saves you the service call.

📞

Call Specialist

This needs a pro. You get told who, why, and how urgent — plus a ready-to-send work order to text a contractor.

⚠️

Danger

Stop. FixGuide flags real hazards before you make it worse. Knows when to keep you safe, not just keep you busy.
