Features

Built for the field.

Everything a field worker or homeowner actually needs — nothing they don't.

🎤

Fully hands-free

Voice-operated end to end. Say "start" to begin, respond with "yes" or describe an issue — your hands never leave the work.

🔄

Adaptive step tracker

One step at a time. If something doesn't work, describe the issue and get an instant fix — not a restart.

📋

Instant work orders

When a specialist is needed, get a plain-language work order ready to text a contractor in seconds.

🌐

Universal

Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, appliances, vehicles, equipment — any physical problem in any domain.

📱

No webcam? No problem

AirDrop a photo from your phone, drop in the path, and get the same full analysis.

🗂

Session history

Every job is logged locally. See your recent sessions on startup — with verdict, description, and timestamp.
